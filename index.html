About Me

I am a physicist working at the intersection of theoretical physics, complex systems and machine learning. My research explores how data-driven methods can be used to uncover the underlying structure and dynamics of high-dimensional physical systems.

My current interests include manifold learning, dimensionality reduction, topological data analysis, explainable artificial intelligence and the application of machine learning to problems in physics.

Research

My research combines concepts from theoretical physics with modern data-driven approaches.

Complex Systems

Dynamical systems, nonlinear phenomena, statistical physics and the emergence of complex behaviour.

Manifold Learning

Understanding the intrinsic geometry and dimensionality of high-dimensional physical trajectories using PCA, t-SNE, autoencoders and related techniques.

Topological Data Analysis

Persistent homology and topological methods for identifying geometric and dynamical structures hidden in complex datasets.

Explainable AI

Developing interpretable and scientifically meaningful machine-learning approaches for physical and scientific applications.
