About Me

I am a physicist working at the intersection of condensed matter physics, complex systems, and AI/ML. My research explores how data-driven methods can be used to uncover the underlying structure and dynamics of high-dimensional physical and chemical systems. I am working freelance, focusing on explainable AI (XAI), and building an IT company to make black-box models transparent and trustworthy.

My current interests include manifold learning, dimensionality reduction, topological data analysis, optimal transport, explainable artificial intelligence and the application of machine learning to scientific problems, e.g., protein spectral data. I am also working on novel approaches to charge transport in materials.

Research

My research combines concepts from theoretical physics with modern data-driven approaches.

Condensed Matter Physics and Dynamical Systems

Many-body interactions in materials, Boltzmann transport equation, Density Functional Theory and Thomas-Fermi potential. Dynamical systems, nonlinear phenomena, statistical physics and the emergence of complex behaviour.

Manifold Learning

Understanding the intrinsic geometry and dimensionality of high-dimensional physical trajectories using PCA, t-SNE, autoencoders and related techniques.

Topological Data Analysis

Persistent homology and topological methods for identifying geometric and dynamical structures hidden in complex datasets.

Explainable AI

Developing interpretable and scientifically meaningful machine-learning approaches for physical and scientific applications.
